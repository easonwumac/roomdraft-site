Support

Getting started

Create a project on a LiDAR iPhone or iPad that supports RoomPlan. Scan each room, then correct dimensions and placement on the floor plan. You can also draw manually on devices without LiDAR.

Scanning, modeling, multiple projects, and sync through your personal iCloud account are free. Pro unlocks the complete PDF drawing set and USDZ exports. The App Store purchase screen shows the current price.

Common questions

Can I build directly from scan dimensions? Measure on site. A qualified professional must review structural, electrical, and plumbing work.

How do I restore a purchase? Tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen using the Apple Account that purchased it.

How do I delete data? Delete the project in the app. If iCloud sync is active, the deletion syncs when you reconnect. You can also manage this app's data in your device's iCloud storage settings.

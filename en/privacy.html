Data processed

The app uses the camera and LiDAR to create indoor space data. Project names, client information, addresses, notes, dimensions, layouts, and scan models you enter are stored on your device. Camera frames are used for scanning and are not streamed to our servers.

iCloud and sharing

When users sign in to iCloud and sync, projects and scans are stored in their Apple iCloud private database for use across their devices. Apple processes iCloud data under its terms and privacy policy. When you export and share a PDF, USDZ, or backup, data goes to the recipient or app you select.

Purchases and support

Apple App Store processes subscriptions. The app uses StoreKit to verify entitlements; it does not receive your payment card number. If you email support, we use the email address and content you provide to reply and resolve the issue.

Retention and deletion

You can delete local projects in the app. For synced projects, the app sends the deletion to iCloud when online. Device or account backups may remain under your Apple settings. You may email us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Other

The app currently has no third-party advertising or analytics SDK. We will update this policy if data practices change. Contact easonwumac@gmail.com with privacy questions.
